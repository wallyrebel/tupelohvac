North Mississippi winter weather can shift quickly, bringing freezing rain, gusty winds, or a brief power outage to Tupelo. A little preparation can help your heating system work under tougher conditions and make it easier to spot trouble early. Use this Tupelo HVAC checklist before a forecast storm, and follow your system’s manufacturer instructions.

## Your pre-storm checklist

- **Check the air filter.** If it looks loaded with dust, replace it with the correct size and type. A clogged filter can restrict airflow.
- **Clear supply and return vents.** Move rugs, furniture, and curtains away so warm air can circulate.
- **Test the thermostat.** Set it to heat and confirm the system responds. Replace weak batteries if your thermostat uses them.
- **Look over the outdoor unit.** Remove loose leaves and branches nearby, but don’t chip ice or snow off equipment or pour hot water on it.
- **Secure outdoor items.** Bring in or tie down lightweight objects that strong winds could blow into the unit.
- **Check alarms and supplies.** Test smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, and keep fresh batteries available.
- **Plan for a power interruption.** Know how to reach your utility provider and where your household can stay warm safely if heat is unavailable.

## When to call for help

If the system won’t start, makes an unusual noise, or blows cool air while set to heat, arrange a professional inspection before severe weather if possible. A burning smell, visible damage, or a carbon monoxide alarm needs prompt attention. Don’t use an oven or outdoor cooking equipment to heat your home.

After a power outage, check your thermostat and breaker only if it’s safe to do so. If a breaker trips repeatedly, leave it off and contact a qualified technician rather than resetting it over and over.

## FAQs

### Should I cover my outdoor heat pump before a storm?
Usually, avoid covering it during operation. A cover can restrict airflow or trap moisture. Follow the manufacturer’s guidance and keep snow, ice, and debris from blocking the unit.

### What temperature should I set during a cold snap?
Choose a comfortable, steady setting that suits your household. If you leave home, avoid setting the temperature so low that pipes could freeze; consider local conditions and your home’s plumbing.
