When your air conditioner runs but the house still feels warm, the problem may be airflow, thermostat settings, or a cooling-system issue. In Tupelo, a sudden warm spell after a cool winter day can make indoor temperatures uncomfortable fast. A few safe checks may help you narrow down what’s happening; if the AC still isn’t cooling, it may be time to fix HVAC Tupelo homeowners rely on for a proper diagnosis.

## Start with these safe checks

Before assuming the system needs repair, try this checklist:

1. **Check the thermostat.** Confirm it’s set to “Cool,” the fan is on “Auto,” and the temperature is below the room temperature.
2. **Inspect the air filter.** A clogged filter can restrict airflow. Replace it if it looks dusty or blocked, using the correct size.
3. **Check supply vents.** Make sure furniture, rugs, or curtains aren’t covering them.
4. **Look at the outdoor unit.** If it’s safe to do so, check whether it’s running and clear away loose leaves or debris around it. Don’t open panels or reach inside.
5. **Notice any ice or unusual sounds.** If you see ice on the indoor coil or refrigerant lines, turn cooling off and contact a technician. Running an iced-up system can add strain.

Give the system some time after making a change. If the air from the vents remains warm or airflow is weak, avoid repeatedly restarting the AC.

## Why an AC may run without cooling well

North Mississippi weather can shift quickly, and even a short warm spell can reveal a problem that wasn’t obvious during colder days. Low airflow, a dirty outdoor coil, electrical trouble, or a refrigerant issue may prevent the system from removing enough heat. Some problems need professional testing; refrigerant and electrical components aren’t safe DIY repairs.

## FAQ

### Should I keep the AC running if it isn’t cooling?
If it blows warm air for an extended period, or you notice ice, shut cooling off. You can use the fan setting if appropriate, but arrange an inspection if the problem continues.

### Can a dirty filter make my home feel warm?
Yes. A clogged filter can limit airflow and reduce comfort. Check it regularly, especially when the system is in frequent use.

### When should I call for service?
Call if basic checks don’t help, the breaker trips, the system makes unusual noises, or cooling remains weak.
