When the summer heat hits Tupelo, MS, a functioning AC system is essential to keep your home comfortable. Understanding common causes and practical troubleshooting steps can help you address the issue promptly. If these tips don’t resolve the problem, consider seeking professional help for a thorough inspection and repair.

## Why Is Your AC Running but Your House Still Feels Warm?

In Tupelo’s humid summers, an AC system working nonstop without cooling effectively can be caused by several issues. It’s important to identify warning signs early to prevent further damage and discomfort.

## Practical Troubleshooting Steps

Follow this checklist to diagnose common problems:

- **Check the Thermostat Settings:** Ensure your thermostat is set to “cool” and at a temperature lower than the current room temperature.
- **Inspect Air Filters:** Dirty filters restrict airflow. Replace or clean filters if they’re clogged.
- **Examine the Outdoor Unit:** Clear debris, leaves, or dirt from the condenser unit. Blockages hinder heat exchange.
- **Ensure Vents Are Open and Unobstructed:** Closed or blocked vents reduce airflow and cooling efficiency.
- **Look for Refrigerant Leaks:** Low refrigerant levels can cause inadequate cooling. Signs include hissing sounds or ice buildup on the coils.
- **Check for Frozen Coils:** Ice on the evaporator coil indicates airflow or refrigerant issues. Turn off the system and allow it to thaw before further inspection.
- **Verify the Ductwork:** Leaks or disconnections in ducts can cause cooled air to escape before reaching living spaces.

## FAQs

**Q: How often should I replace my air filters in Tupelo’s humid climate?**  
A: Check filters monthly during summer. Replace them every 1-3 months, especially if you notice dirt or reduced airflow.

**Q: Can duct leaks cause my AC to run but not cool effectively?**  
A: Yes, leaks allow cooled air to escape, reducing efficiency. Sealing ducts can improve cooling performance.

**Q: When should I call a professional for AC repair?**  
A: If basic troubleshooting doesn’t restore proper cooling, or if you notice refrigerant issues, strange noises, or leaks, contact a professional promptly.
