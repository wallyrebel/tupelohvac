## Why Fall HVAC Prep Matters in Tupelo

Tupelo’s weather can be unpredictable in the fall, with thunderstorms and high winds common. Storms can cause power surges, debris damage, or moisture intrusion, all of which threaten your air conditioning system’s performance. Preparing your HVAC system now helps prevent costly repairs and keeps your home comfortable when storms roll through.

## Fall Pre-Storm HVAC Checklist

Use this practical checklist to ready your air conditioner for storm season:

- **Inspect & Clear Outdoor Unit:** Remove leaves, branches, and debris around the condenser. Ensure at least 2 feet of clearance for airflow.
- **Check for Damage:** Look for dents, rust, or loose parts. If you notice any issues, consider scheduling a professional service.
- **Secure Electrical Connections:** Make sure all wiring is intact and connections are tight. Consider installing surge protectors to guard against power surges.
- **Change or Clean Filters:** A clean filter improves efficiency and reduces strain during storm-related power fluctuations.
- **Test the System:** Turn on your AC to verify it’s functioning correctly. Listen for unusual noises or reduced airflow.
